# Planet texture credits

These low-resolution equirectangular maps are bundled for the ShipOS planetary display. They contain no NASA logos or identifiable people and do not imply NASA endorsement.

- `earth-blue-marble-1024.png` — NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Blue Marble; Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stöckli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA Earth Observatory. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/
- `moon-lro-1024.jpg` — NASA Scientific Visualization Studio; LROC WAC color mosaic from NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
- `mars-viking-1024.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech; Viking imagery processed by USGS. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/
- `europa-voyager-1024.jpg` — USGS, JPL and Caltech; Voyager imagery mosaic. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/jupiter-europa/
- `venus-magellan-1024.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech; Magellan radar imagery. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/venus/

Mars, Europa and Venus were resampled to 1024×512 JPEGs for this application. NASA's media guidance says NASA content and 3D texture-map media are generally not subject to copyright in the United States, while requiring factual use, source acknowledgement and no implied endorsement: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Fictional and unmatched ShipOS bodies continue to use deterministic procedural textures rather than being represented as a specific real Solar System body.
